Library project — initial requirements

1. A student shall be able to search books by title or author.
2. The system should respond quickly.
3. A librarian shall be able to add and update book records.
4. Borrowing records shall be visible only to the relevant student and librarian.

Discussion points for a later AI review:
- What response time is acceptable for book searches?
- Can students reserve a book that is already on loan?

This Sprint 1 prototype validates and previews the text. It does not analyze it yet.
